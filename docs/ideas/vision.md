# Finance Platform

## Vision

Syncing all our shared finances with my dad has become a struggle. All the transactions are hard to evaluate, as they happen on different banks and even in different currencies. Also they are not periodical and therefore hard to track, and this must be done manually. However, typing all the details into some Excel on a laptop is an annoying task, which is easy to forget too.

Even though it's not as hard to implement, an idea of making some app for a phone which would be accessible from anywhere in the world sounds both like a convenient solution for a problem and as a nice side project to do.

Moving from this starting point, I also came across an idea that the app might be scaled a little at least to the size of our family, where everyone will be able to privately track their finances if needed. Another extension is a possibility of creating groups for trips or some other possible multi-transaction events.

---

## Problem Statement

### What problem does it solve?

Transactions are messy and hard to track. Here they are accumulated in one place and some key values are calculated autonomously.

---

## Target Audience

### Who's it for?

- Me
- Dad
- Probably sister
- Andrea, her wife, if they will want to use it for some of their stuff
- Also mom if she goes to some trip with us

---

## User Experience

### What shall the user feel about the app?

It must be extremely easy to use, and 3-5 clicks shall add a transaction in seconds.
